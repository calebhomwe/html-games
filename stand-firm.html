Your watch

Escort at least two civilians to the water. Select a defender, then tap an outlined tile or a raider. Tap a civilian to check their escort status.

How to play

Each defender has 2 actions. Moving one tile or attacking costs one action. Attack once per turn. Warden and Guard strike adjacent raiders; Ranger shoots up to 3 tiles in a straight, unobstructed line.

At the end of your turn, civilians next to a defender step toward the water only when no raider is adjacent; then raiders move up to 2 tiles or strike. Brace costs an action and blocks 1 damage until your next turn. Rescue 2 people before turn 9; three watches win the campaign.
